Faucet and sink repair in Florissant, MO

Faucet and sink repair in Florissant, MO covers kitchen, bathroom, utility and tub faucets, the supply lines and shutoff valves below, and the sink drain and trap. A small drip is easy to ignore, but a faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year.

We repair what is worth repairing and replace what is not. Many faucets have cartridge or ceramic disc parts that can be swapped in minutes, and a sink leak under a cabinet is frequently a loose slip nut or a worn supply line, not a failed faucet.

Faucet emergency: the valve will not shut off

A faucet that will not stop running, a supply line that has split or a shutoff valve that has failed can flood a kitchen quickly. Close the shutoff valves under the sink by turning them clockwise. If they are stuck or leaking, close the main water valve for the house and call us.

Do not force a corroded valve. Old stop valves can crack and make the leak worse, and replacing a failed valve is routine work for us.

  • Turn both under-sink shutoffs clockwise.
  • If a valve will not turn, close the main water valve.
  • Empty the cabinet and photograph any damage.
  • Place a bucket under the trap and call.

What usually fails in a faucet

Mineral scale in hard water roughens seats and clogs aerators. Missouri American Water's 2023 report lists total hardness up to 226 ppm at its Missouri River plants, so cleaning aerators and replacing worn cartridges is a normal part of keeping a faucet healthy here.

Cartridge or disc

Wear inside the valve body is behind most single handle drips.

Washers and seats

Older compression faucets leak when rubber washers harden.

Supply lines

Braided lines under the sink fail at the fittings and at age.

Shutoff valves

Old multi-turn stop valves seize and drip at the stem.

Sink drains and supply lines

A leak under the sink is often at the trap, the garbage disposal connection or the drain basket. We tighten, reseal or replace the part and run water to test. If the sink is clogged, drain cleaning is the next stop, and a disposal leak is handled by our garbage disposal repair team.

Common faucet and sink problems we see

Many of these are quick fixes. A worn washer, a cartridge or a supply line can be changed in one visit.

  • A handle that is hard to turn or will not shut off.
  • A spray head that sprays sideways or leaks at the hose.
  • Low flow caused by a clogged aerator.
  • A leak at the shutoff valve or supply line.
  • A drain that is slow because of the pop-up assembly.

Shutoff valves that do not shut off

If a faucet will not stop and the shutoff valve below will not close, you have an emergency, because the main water valve is the only control left. Find the main valve before you need it and try turning it a quarter turn once a year so it does not seize.

Old shutoff valves can fail when moved, which is why we replace them during a faucet repair when we see corrosion. A new valve costs little compared with a flooded cabinet.
