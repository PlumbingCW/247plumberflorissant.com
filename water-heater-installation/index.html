Water heater installation in Florissant, MO

Water heater installation in Florissant, MO is a planned job, and planning is where most of the savings are. You pick the day, the unit and the fuel, and the install is checked against the venting, gas line and water connections that are already in your home.

This page is for installs you control, such as a new construction hookup, a move from electric to gas, an upgrade to a larger tank or a planned swap before the old tank fails. If your tank is already leaking, our emergency replacement service is the faster path.

What a proper installation includes

The relief line and venting are the items we see skipped most often on poor installs, and both are safety items rather than cosmetic ones.

  • Correct tank size for the number of people and bathrooms in the home.
  • New supply connectors, shutoff valve and a dielectric fitting where metals differ.
  • Temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line run to a safe drain point.
  • Venting checked and, for gas units, a combustion and leak check at the connection.
  • Expansion tank where the plumbing is a closed system.
  • Drain pan under the tank where the layout allows it.
  • Permit paperwork and a walk-through of the shutoffs.

Florissant permit requirements

The City of Florissant requires a residential permit for plumbing work and water heater replacement, handled through the Public Works department. We pull the permit as part of an install and schedule any city inspection with you. If a permit is needed, we will say so at the quote stage, not on the day. Contact Public Works at the city if you are not sure what applies to your address.

Why local water matters for the install

Missouri American Water's 2023 report for the St. Louis County system lists total hardness of 82 to 226 ppm at its Missouri River plants. Hard water leaves scale on heating surfaces, so we talk about a flush schedule and, if you want to cut scale at the source, a water softener installed upstream of the heater.

The same report notes the water is treated with chloramines and pH is high from lime softening, which is another reason to use quality connectors and fittings.

Preparing for installation day

A clear workspace makes the job faster and lowers the chance of damage. We bring the new tank, fittings and a drain pan where one is needed.

  • Clear a path to the heater and the area around it.
  • Tell us about any past leaks or old repairs near the heater.
  • Plan for water to be off for a few hours.
  • Decide whether you want a smaller or larger tank than before.

Safety checklist before the plumber arrives

Safety comes first on gas and electric work.

  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  • Keep the area around the heater clear.
  • Turn off power or fuel only as instructed.
  • Keep children away from hot water lines.
