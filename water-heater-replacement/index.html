Water heater replacement in Florissant, MO

Water heater replacement in Florissant, MO covers removing a failed tank and setting a new one that fits your household. We reconnect gas or electric, water, venting and the relief line, then test everything before we leave.

A typical tank lasts roughly a decade, and the ones in the oldest Florissant homes have often been replaced once or twice already. If yours is leaking from the body, rusting at the base or more than 12 years old and failing, replacement is the sound choice.

Emergency tank failure: stop the water first

A burst or leaking tank can put a lot of water on a basement floor in an hour. Call us at any time, and in the meantime shut the cold supply valve above the tank. If the heater is electric, turn off its breaker before you step into any water.

Gas units need the gas control turned to off. If you smell gas at any point, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

  • Cold supply valve above the tank: turn clockwise to close.
  • Electric heater: breaker off before anyone touches the unit.
  • Gas heater: control to off, and leave if you smell gas.
  • Move boxes and appliances off the wet floor.

What happens on replacement day

Most replacements finish in a few hours. Tight utility closets and older venting add time, and we say so before we start.

Size and fuel match

We confirm tank size against your household and check venting before choosing a unit.

Drain and remove

The old tank is drained, disconnected and carried out.

Set and connect

New unit, new supply connectors, new relief line and a drain pan where space allows.

Test and explain

We fire the unit, check for leaks and show you the shutoffs.

Florissant permit rules for replacement

The City of Florissant lists water heater replacement among the residential work that requires a permit through Public Works. We plan the permit into the job so the new heater is documented and inspected where required. If you are on a tight timeline, tell us at the start so we can sequence the visit around the city's hours.
