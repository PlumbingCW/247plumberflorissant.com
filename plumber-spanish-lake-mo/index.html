Neighborhood first: an unincorporated community

Spanish Lake is an unincorporated community and census-designated place in northeastern St. Louis County. It is bounded by the Mississippi River to the east, the Missouri River to the north, Highway 367 to the west and Interstate 270 to the south. The 2020 Census counted 18,413 people and 8,862 housing units.

Because it has no city government of its own, permit and inspection questions may be handled by St. Louis County and not a city office, so ask which applies before work begins.

A long local history, a mid-century housing base

The name comes from a lake once called Spanish Pond, and the area grew around Fort Bellefontaine, established in 1806. German farmers settled around the Bellefontaine, Parker and Spanish Pond road intersection, and tract housing arrived in the 1950s with apartments added in the 1970s.

Homes from the 1950s tract era have plumbing nearly seventy years old. We see aging drain lines and water heaters, which is why sewer camera inspection and water heater replacement come up often.
