Sewer line cleaning in Florissant, MO

Sewer line cleaning in Florissant, MO is the work of clearing the main building drain and the lateral that leads out to the street. Cleaning removes grease, paper, roots and debris that narrow the pipe, so waste flows at full speed again.

The lateral is the longest and deepest pipe in your plumbing system, and a clog there affects every fixture. That is why we clean from a cleanout whenever one exists, because it gives direct access without pulling a toilet.

Sewage backup: emergency steps before we arrive

A sewer backup into the lowest drain is an emergency. We take calls around the clock. Turn off the water heater if floodwater is near it, stop using water and keep everyone out of the affected area.

If the cleanout cap is outside, do not open it yourself, because pressure may release wastewater. Leave the cap for our technician, who will have a catch and the tools to control the flow.

Cleaning methods for the main line

We start with a drum cable machine matched to the pipe diameter. Cable heads cut through roots and soft clogs and are the right first choice for many blockages.

If the line holds grease, scale or roots that regrow quickly, hydro jetting cleans the full pipe wall. If cleaning does not hold, a camera inspection reveals whether a break or low spot is the cause.

Why cleaning is not always a one-time fix

A sewer line with roots is a repeating problem unless the entry point is repaired. Clay joints in older pipe open slightly over decades and roots find them. A cleaning clears the symptom for months, but the roots return.

Florissant also has mature trees along many residential streets. The city recommends annual cabling if large trees or shrubs are near the sewer line, which is a useful maintenance rhythm for homes with a root history.

How often a main line needs cleaning

A healthy line may go years between visits. A line with mature trees near it, an old clay or cast iron pipe or a history of backups may need cleaning on a yearly schedule. We can set a reminder after the first visit based on what the camera finds.

If the same line backs up again within weeks of cleaning, the line itself is probably damaged. At that point cleaning is a short-term measure and a sewer camera inspection shows what repair is needed.
