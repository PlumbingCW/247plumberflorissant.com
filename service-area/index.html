Where Florissant 24/7 Plumbing works

We dispatch from Florissant, ZIP 63031, and cover the towns around it in north St. Louis County. The neighboring ZIP codes 63032, 63033 and 63034 fall within our core Florissant area, and each town below has its own page with local notes.

The same 24/7 emergency line serves every town on this page. A technician is assigned by location and job type, and you get a clear price before work starts. If you are not sure whether your address is in the area, call and the dispatcher will confirm in a minute.

Hazelwood, MO

ZIP 63042. Hazelwood lies north of St. Louis-Lambert International Airport and is crossed by Interstates 270 and 170, Lindbergh Boulevard and Highway 370. Homes, shops and light industry share the same pipes and the same emergency line.

Ferguson, MO

ZIP 63135. Ferguson began in 1855 when a railroad depot was built here, and the city incorporated in 1894. Its older streets mean older drains, and the Florissant Valley campus of St. Louis Community College brings rentals with frequent turnover.

Black Jack, MO

ZIP 63033. Black Jack incorporated on August 6, 1970 and shares ZIP 63033 with Florissant. Its Public Works office issues plumbing permits to master plumbers and drain layers licensed by St. Louis County, so permit steps differ from Florissant.

Bridgeton, MO

ZIP 63044. Bridgeton borders the Missouri River and includes part of the airport. Sump pumps, floor drains and backwater protection matter more where heavy rain and river-driven water tables meet.

Spanish Lake, MO

ZIP 63138. Spanish Lake is an unincorporated community in northeastern St. Louis County, so permits go through the county. Its mid-century tract homes often have drains and supply lines that are reaching old age.

Neighborhoods inside Florissant

Two Florissant neighborhoods have dedicated pages: Old Town, the historic center, and Paddock Hills, a postwar ranch-home subdivision off Parker Road. They are parts of Florissant, not separate towns.

How to tell which town page fits you

If your address is in Florissant itself, the services pages and the home page cover you. If you are in one of the five neighboring towns, open the matching page for local permit notes, the ZIP codes we cover and a short checklist for that area. If your mail uses a different city name than your actual town, go by the street address and the ZIP code table above, or call and the dispatcher will confirm in a minute.

Every town page lists the same core services, because a burst pipe in Hazelwood is the same problem as one in Ferguson. The differences are in permits, housing age and the things that typically go wrong in that area, and those differences are what each page explains.

What changes from town to town

Plumbing problems follow housing age, soil and local rules. Older streets in Florissant and Ferguson tend to have cast iron drains and original water heater closets. Newer subdivisions in Black Jack may have different permit rules and homeowner association limits on contractors. Near the Missouri River in Bridgeton, groundwater and floor drains deserve extra attention. Our plumbing services pages explain the work, and each town page explains the local details.

Permits are one of the biggest differences. The City of Florissant lists plumbing among residential projects that need a permit through Public Works. Black Jack issues permits to licensed master plumbers and drain layers. Spanish Lake is unincorporated, so St. Louis County handles it. When you book a water heater, repipe or remodel job, we tell you which office applies to your address.
