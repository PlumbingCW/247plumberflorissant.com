Drain cleaning for Florissant homes

Drain cleaning in Florissant, MO means removing whatever is blocking a fixture drain or branch line and then confirming that water moves freely before we leave. A clog is a symptom. Grease, hair, wipes and roots are the usual causes, and a proper visit names which one it was.

Emergency drain clog: what to do first

A drain that is fully blocked and rising is an emergency, especially when it is the lowest drain in the house. We take calls at night and on weekends, and a plumber can be put on the schedule as soon as a crew is free.

While you wait, stop all water use in the house, because every flush and shower adds to what is already stuck. Keep children and pets out of any standing water, and do not mix drain chemicals, since they can burn skin and make the next step dangerous for the technician.

  • Turn off the washing machine and dishwasher so they stop feeding the line.
  • If water is coming up in the lowest tub or floor drain, the main line is likely blocked, so tell us that on the call.
  • Move rugs and electronics away from the wet area.
  • Skip the plunger on a toilet that is also backing up a tub, because the blockage is further down.

Signs the clog is in the main line

One slow sink is a branch problem. Several fixtures acting up together points to the main drain. Pay attention to the pattern before you call, because it tells us which tool to bring.

  • A toilet flush makes the tub or shower gurgle.
  • Water backs up into the lowest drain in the basement when someone runs the washer.
  • Two or more fixtures drain slowly at the same time.
  • A sewer smell near a floor drain that did not used to be there.
  • The clog returns within weeks of a plunger fix or a bottle of drain cleaner.

How we clear and confirm the line

We start with the least invasive tool that will work. A hand auger handles a sink trap or short branch. A motorized drain cable reaches the main line and cuts through soft blockages and light roots. When the cable only punches a hole, we recommend hydro jetting to scrub the pipe wall.

If a line clogs again soon after cleaning, we run a sewer camera inspection so you can see a belly, a cracked joint or root entry before you pay for more cleaning. Seeing the pipe is the difference between a repair and an endless cycle.
