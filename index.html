When to call the emergency plumber right now

Some problems can wait for a normal appointment, and some cannot. Call the 24/7 line immediately for any of the following, and ask about timing for the rest.

  • Water spraying or flowing from a pipe you cannot shut off.
  • Sewage backing up through a floor drain, tub or toilet.
  • A water heater leaking heavily or smelling of gas. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.
  • No water at all, with a pipe that may have frozen or split.
  • Water near outlets, panels or appliances.

Dripping faucets, slow drains with no backup and a toilet that runs but flushes can usually wait for a scheduled appointment. If you are not sure, call anyway. Describing the problem to a dispatcher costs nothing and helps you decide.

Local things that shape plumbing in Florissant

Missouri American Water reports total hardness up to 226 ppm at its Missouri River plants, which is hard water. Scale builds on heating elements and inside tank water heaters, so flushing and an anode rod check matter here. A water softener is a real option for homes that fight scale.

According to the City of Florissant, its Sewer Lateral Insurance Program covers defective residential sewer laterals from the main to within five feet of the foundation. It is funded by a $50 annual fee and a $300 refundable deposit, so it is worth reading before you pay for a lateral repair. Our sewer camera inspection gives you a recording to bring to that conversation.

The city also lists plumbing work among residential projects that need a permit through Public Works. Ask us about the permit step on any water heater, repiping or new fixture job and we will tell you what applies.
