Rough-in and finish work

New plumbing happens in two stages. Rough-in puts drain, vent and supply lines in the walls and floor before they are closed up. Finish work sets fixtures such as toilets, sinks and tubs once the surfaces are done. A mistake in the rough-in is hard to fix later, so we check locations and slopes against the plans first.

Drains need a steady slope so waste moves without pooling, and vents need a path to the roof so water flows without trapping air. Supply lines need shutoff valves at each fixture, which pay off the first time something leaks.
