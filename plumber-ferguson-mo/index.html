Older housing first: what Ferguson's history means for pipes

Ferguson began in 1855 when William B. Ferguson deeded 10 acres to the Wabash Railroad for a depot, and it became the first station directly connected to St. Louis. The city incorporated in 1894 and built its first schoolhouse in 1878. That is a century earlier than Florissant's postwar subdivision growth, so the age range of homes here is wider.

The 2020 Census counted 8,887 housing units in Ferguson. Plumbing checklist for Ferguson landlords

Rental units turn over often, and each turnover is a chance to catch small problems before they become tenant calls. A short walk-through of the plumbing at each move-out costs little and avoids emergency visits later.

We work with property managers who want a single number for after-hours calls and a written report after each visit. Our drain cleaning and toilet repair crews handle the most frequent rental calls.

  • Run every faucet and flush every toilet at move-out.
  • Look under sinks and behind toilets for staining.
  • Test the water heater temperature and relief valve.
  • Note slow drains for cleaning before the next tenant.
  • Photograph meter reading and shutoff locations.
