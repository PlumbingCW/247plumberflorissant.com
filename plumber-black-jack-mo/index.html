Start with permits: Black Jack works differently

Black Jack's Public Works office issues plumbing permits to master plumbers and drain layers licensed by St. Louis County. The city also notes that water heater permits can go to any qualified individual, and that a homeowner may obtain plumbing or electrical permits for a single-family home they occupy, after buying the equipment, passing a short code test and scheduling inspections.

That flexibility is useful, but it shifts work onto you. If you would rather have a plumber handle the paperwork and inspections, ask about that when you call. City Hall is at 12500 Old Jamestown Road, and applications should be filed a few days before work. Confirm current rules with the city, since they can change.

Source: City of Black Jack permits page.

A small city with a long road history

Black Jack was incorporated on August 6, 1970, covering about 1,700 acres. Its name comes from blackjack oak trees at the crossing of Parker and Old Halls Ferry Roads that travelers used as a landmark by the 1840s. The 2020 Census counted 6,634 residents.

Because the city incorporated in the 1970s but many houses date from earlier decades, plumbing ages here vary lot by lot. We check what you have before quoting. Water heater repair and drain cleaning are our two busiest calls in the area.

Subdivision plumbing, house by house

In subdivisions built in a short span, neighbors often share the same layout, materials and problems. A pattern of slow drains on one street can mean a shared sewer lateral or similar age pipes. We note what we see and tell you if the same problem may apply to the house next door.

For repeated backups, our sewer line cleaning visit with a camera shows whether the line is clogged or damaged.

Water heater and drain calls in Black Jack

Black Jack homes were built over several decades, and the water heaters in them range from new tanks to units well past their expected life. A tank that is rusting at the seams or making popping sounds is giving a warning. Our water heater repair visit can tell you whether to repair or replace, and a drain that keeps slowing is a job for drain cleaning.

Safety checklist before the plumber arrives

Use this short list while the crew is on the way.

  • Shut off the main water valve.
  • Keep away from wet outlets and panels.
  • Stop using drains that are backing up.
  • Photograph damage for insurance.

Schools and subdivisions

Black Jack sits in the Hazelwood School District, and most of its homes are in subdivisions managed by trustees or homeowners associations, which sometimes have rules about exterior work and contractor access. If your subdivision has trustees, tell us so we can plan around any permit or access steps.
