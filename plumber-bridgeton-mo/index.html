Why backwater protection matters here

A backwater valve lets sewage flow out of your house but closes if the sewer backs up toward it. In an area with heavy rain and floodplain, this can keep the lowest drain from filling with sewage during a storm. It needs to be installed correctly and tested, and it needs occasional cleaning. Ask about a valve at the next sewer inspection.

Safety checklist before the plumber arrives

Flooded basements carry electrical and contamination risks.

  • Move valuables to a higher level.
  • Call the line, and call 911 for any danger to people.
