A Hazelwood economy built around industry and homes

Hazelwood sits north of St. Louis-Lambert International Airport and is crossed by Interstates 270 and 170, Lindbergh Boulevard and Highway 370. For decades its identity was tied to work. A Ford plant operated here from 1948 to 2006 and employed 1,400 people when it closed, and the site is being redeveloped as the Aviator Business Park.

That history shapes the plumbing calls we see. Hazelwood has a large base of homes from the postwar years alongside warehouses, logistics centers and light industrial buildings, such as the Hazelwood Logistics Center with 1.8 million square feet of space. We handle both kinds of calls, and a drain or leak at a small business is treated as seriously as one at a home.

What Hazelwood calls about most

For homes, the usual calls are clogged main lines, aging water heaters and leaks in older supply piping. For businesses, restroom failures, grease and floor-drain backups are common. Water heater trouble in Hazelwood homes

Hazelwood has many homes with the original water heater location in a basement or utility closet. When a tank ages, it may leak slowly at the base long before it floods. Check for rust at the bottom and for damp patches. If the tank is more than about a decade old and starts to leak, replacement is usually the sensible route, and our water heater replacement crew handles the swap, the haul-away and the permit steps.
