Sewer camera inspection in Florissant, MO

A sewer camera inspection in Florissant, MO sends a waterproof camera on a flexible rod through your cleanout or a pulled toilet to record the inside of the lateral line. It shows cracks, offset joints, roots, grease buildup and low spots where water pools.

Because most Florissant homes were built in the postwar boom, with the city's population rising from 1,369 in 1940 to 38,166 in 1960, lines of 60 years or more are common. An inspection tells you whether yours is aging gracefully or heading for failure.

When an inspection is worth it

Pre-purchase scopes are one of the best uses, since a replaced lateral is a large expense to find out about after closing.

  • Buying a house and the seller has no sewer records.
  • A main line has clogged more than once in a year.
  • You have a soft spot or sinkhole in the yard.
  • A cable or jetter would not hold the line open.
  • A tree is growing near the lateral.
  • You want a baseline before applying to the city lateral program.

Buying or selling a home? Call and ask about a camera scope.

Using inspection results with the city program

The City of Florissant's Sewer Lateral Insurance Program has its own city plumber video inspection, which is done by the city once an application is approved. Our recorded run is not a substitute. It helps you decide whether to apply and gives you a record of the line before and after any work. Program rules and fees are posted by the city and can change.

What the camera shows and what it does not

A sewer camera shows the inside of the pipe in real time. It finds roots, cracks, offsets, bellies where water sits, scale and breaks. It does not by itself show the pipe's exact depth, but a locator on the camera head can mark the surface above a problem so a repair crew knows where to dig.

Water in a belly can hide the pipe floor, and a very heavy blockage may need cleaning before the camera can pass. We note those limits in the report so you know what was and was not seen.

When an inspection pays for itself

Our sewer line repair page explains the options a recording leads to, and drain cleaning covers simple clogs a camera may help locate.

  • Before buying an older house with mature trees.
  • After a second or third backup in a year.
  • Before paying for a lateral repair or lining.
  • When the city sinkhole or the program application needs documentation.

Older neighborhoods and a camera look

A camera is most useful where the line is old and the records are thin. Our neighborhood pages for Old Town and Paddock Hills explain what is documented about those areas and what stays unknown until someone looks.
