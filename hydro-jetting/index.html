What hydro jetting does for a Florissant sewer line

Hydro jetting in Florissant, MO sends water at very high pressure through a hose with a rotating nozzle to scrub the inside of a drain or sewer line. A cable cuts a channel through a clog and leaves the coating on the pipe wall. A jetter removes that coating, so the line behaves like a clean pipe instead of a narrowed one.

This matters in a city where many homes were built between the mid 1950s and 1960s. Cast iron and clay lines of that age develop scale and rough joints where grease and roots hold on, which is why a single cable pass is often a short fix.

Emergency main line backup: first steps

A main line that backs up into a tub or basement drain is an emergency because the water has nowhere else to go. Call us day or night. If the line has been snaked recently and failed, say so, since that pushes us toward jetting with a camera rather than another cable pass.

Until we arrive, keep every fixture off. Do not run the washer, even for a rinse cycle. Photograph the water level so you have a record for any insurance conversation.

  • Shut off the water heater if floor water is rising near it.
  • Close the basement door to children and pets.
  • Do not pour solvent or bleach into the drain.
  • Note when each fixture last drained normally.

When jetting is the right call and when it is not

Jetting is a strong fit for grease buildup, heavy scale, soap and hair mats deep in a line, and fine root hair that a cable only shreds. It is not the right tool for a collapsed or badly offset pipe, because pressure can push debris into a gap and make the problem worse.

That is why we put a camera into the line first when the history suggests damage. If the picture shows a break, we move to a sewer line repair quote instead of washing out a pipe that cannot hold.

Signs your line is ready for jetting

A line that shows those signs usually responds well. We confirm by running the camera again after the job so you see the cleaned pipe.

  • Three or more clogs in the past year at the same fixture.
  • A cable returns with grease or scale on the cutting head.
  • Slow drains return within weeks of cleaning.
  • The camera shows a rough, narrowed wall rather than a broken pipe.

Safety checklist before the plumber arrives

These steps keep the visit safe and the work fast.

  • Stop running water into the affected drain.
  • Keep children and pets away from backed-up areas.
  • Do not mix chemical cleaners, since they can splash back during jetting.
  • Clear access to the cleanout.

Jetting after a sewer lateral problem

Florissant's Sewer Lateral Insurance Program asks that a licensed plumber has tried and failed to cable the line before the city video inspects it. A jetting visit does not replace that step, but our camera notes can help you document what the pipe looks like. Ask the city's Public Works engineering office what it needs for your address.
