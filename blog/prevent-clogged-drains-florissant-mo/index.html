How to Prevent Clogged Drains in Florissant, MO Homes

Most clogs are built over months by habits. Change the habits and you change the calls.

By the Florissant 24/7 Plumbing team, 2026.

Call (314) 461-6239

Kitchen: fat, oil and food scraps

Grease cools and hardens in the pipe, and hot water only moves it further down. Wipe pans with a paper towel and put the towel in the trash. Strain food scraps from the sink basket, even with a disposal, because fibrous and starchy scraps build into mats.

Run cold water for a few seconds after using the disposal so the debris travels beyond the trap and the first section of line.

Bathroom: hair and soap

A hair catcher in the tub and shower costs little and stops the most common bathroom clog. Soap scum and hair knit together into a mass that a plunger will not move. Pull the stopper and clean it every month or two.

Never flush wipes, cotton products or dental floss. Wipes labeled flushable still do not break down like paper.

When the clog is a pipe problem

If clogs repeat despite careful habits, the pipe may be the cause. Roots, scale and a sag in the line can hold material no matter how careful you are. A camera inspection shows which, and hydro jetting cleans scale and light roots from the wall.

Kitchen drains

Grease is the main kitchen enemy. Hot grease runs as a liquid, then cools and coats the inside of the pipe, catching food and building a plug over months. Wipe pans with a paper towel and put the towel in the trash, and keep a can for cooking fat.

A garbage disposal grinds scraps but does not make them disappear. Coffee grounds, eggshells, pasta and rice swell or pack in the trap. Run cold water for several seconds before and after using the disposal so scraps move past the trap and into the larger drain.

Bathroom drains

The City of Florissant lists grease, oils and wipes among the items to keep out of drains, and that advice protects your own lateral before it protects the city main.

  • Fit a hair catcher over the tub and shower drain.
  • Clear the catcher after each use, not once a month.
  • Use only toilet paper in toilets. Wipes sold as flushable still tangle and clog.
  • Pull the stopper and clean the pivot rod arm in bathroom sinks every few months.

A simple yearly routine

Repeated slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line, and that is a drain cleaning or sewer line cleaning call rather than a plunger job.

Monthly

Flush kitchen drains with very hot tap water, not boiling water on PVC.

Each season

Clean pop-up stoppers and hair catchers.

Every year

Cable or camera-check a main line with a history of trouble.

Near trees

Watch for slow drains and gurgling that signal roots.

When to stop DIY and call

A slow drain that clears with a plunger and stays clear is a small matter. A drain that clears and returns within days, or several fixtures draining slowly at once, points to a partial blockage deeper in the line. Pouring more chemical cleaner into that kind of problem rarely works and can damage old pipes.

If water comes up in the lowest tub or floor drain when you run the washer, call the same day. That is the pattern of a main line backup, and it can bring sewage into the house.

Why trees and roots matter

Tree roots grow toward moisture, and a sewer lateral with a loose joint is an invitation. Large trees can send roots a long way, so a clog that keeps returning near mature trees often points to roots. A camera confirms the cause and tells you whether cutting the roots is enough or the pipe needs repair.

The City of Florissant recommends annual cabling when large trees or shrubs are near the lateral, and that is sensible advice. A yearly visit costs less than an emergency backup, and it gives you a record of how the line is changing.

What about chemical cleaners

Liquid drain cleaners can open a slow drain, but they are harsh on old pipes and dangerous if they splash. They also leave the pipe full of caustic liquid, which then hits a plumber's hands and eyes if the clog needs mechanical removal. If you use one, follow the label, never mix products and do not use one in a toilet that is full.

A drain that needs repeated chemicals has a physical problem. A cable or camera is the next step, and it is safer for the pipe.

What to know in Florissant

The City of Florissant tells residents not to flush or pour wipes, paper towels, medication, grease or oils into drains, and it recommends annual cabling when large trees or shrubs are near the sewer line. Those two points are the core of a Florissant maintenance plan.

The city also runs a Sewer Lateral Insurance Program for defective residential laterals, which requires proof that the line was cabled by a licensed plumber without success, so keep records of every service visit.

These habits help just as much in Ferguson and Spanish Lake.
