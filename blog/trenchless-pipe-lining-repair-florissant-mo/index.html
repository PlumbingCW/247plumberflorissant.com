Trenchless Pipe Lining Repair for Florissant, MO Homes

Lining repairs a pipe from the inside. It is a strong tool for the right pipe, and a poor one for the wrong pipe.

By the Florissant 24/7 Plumbing team, 2026.

Call (314) 461-6239

How lining works

A flexible liner saturated with resin is pulled or inverted into the old pipe and inflated against the walls. The resin hardens, and the result is a pipe inside the pipe. It seals cracks and open joints and blocks root entry.

The process needs a clean pipe, so cleaning and a camera pass come first. Access is usually through a cleanout and one small excavation, which is why landscaping stays largely in place.

What suits lining and what does not

Pipe conditionLining?Why
Cracks, open joints, root entryYesThe liner seals them.
Mild corrosion and scaleOften, after cleaningClean wall required.
Collapsed or crushed sectionNoLiner cannot pass.
Large sag with standing waterUsually noLiner follows the sag.
Offset joint that blocks the toolNoExcavation needed.

Questions to ask a lining contractor

A good contractor shows you the footage and explains why lining fits. If the line needs more than lining, they should say so. Start with a sewer camera inspection.

  • What is the liner material and its stated lifespan?
  • What cleaning is done before lining?
  • Will you show me the camera before and after?
  • How are lateral connections reopened?
  • What is the warranty and who stands behind it?

Lining compared with digging

Digging removes the old pipe and replaces it, which is the most reliable fix for collapsed or bellied lines. Lining is less disruptive and often faster, but it does not correct a low spot. We discuss both and price both in the plan. If scale is the main problem, hydro jetting may be enough for now.

Preparing a pipe for lining

Preparation decides the result. The pipe has to be cleaned of roots, scale and grease, usually by jetting and cutters, so the liner bonds to a clean wall. A camera then checks the cleaned pipe for collapses, offsets and low spots that would stop the liner.

If the camera shows a problem the liner cannot cover, the plan changes before anyone has mixed resin. That is why we ask for the inspection first and do not quote lining blind. See sewer line cleaning for the cleaning step by itself.

What a recorded inspection should show

A recording lets you compare quotes and keep the evidence if you apply to the city program. You can ask us for a copy.

  • The pipe material and diameter.
  • Locations of cracks, open joints and root entry.
  • Any sag where water sits.
  • The location of the cleanout and the tie-in to the main.

Questions homeowners ask us

If your line needs more than lining, we explain replacement options on our sewer line repair page.

Will the lawn survive?

Lining usually needs one small access pit, so most of the yard stays as it is.

Does it block lateral connections?

The liner is cut open at connections after curing, which is why a camera is used at the end.

Can the city program help?

The City of Florissant program covers defective residential laterals within its terms. Ask Public Works whether lining is accepted.

How long does it take?

Many jobs are completed in a day, with the cure time depending on the product.

Comparing lining quotes

Quotes should state the length of pipe lined, the number of access points and the cleaning included. They should name the liner material and say whether the lateral connections are reopened and tested. A quote that is a single number with no details is hard to compare.

Ask each contractor for a before and after recording and a written warranty. If the contractor will not provide those, you do not know what you are buying.

Aftercare and the first year

After lining, flow is smoother and root entry at joints is blocked. You can run water normally once the cure is complete. We suggest a follow-up camera pass after a year if the line had a history of problems, so you can see that the liner is holding and the connections are open.

Keep the cleanout accessible and avoid planting trees directly over the line. If you ever see slow drains return, call early so the cause can be checked before it grows.

Where lining fits in a repair plan

Lining often sits between a simple cleaning and a full replacement. If the pipe is sound but leaking at joints, lining stops the leak and the root entry. If the pipe has collapsed in one spot, a spot repair plus lining on either side is sometimes the answer. If the pipe is broken or badly bellied, replacement is the right choice.

We choose the method on the recording and explain why. If another contractor recommends lining and your pipe is bellied, ask them how the liner will fix the low spot.

What to know in Florissant

Florissant's Sewer Lateral Insurance Program covers defective residential laterals from the main to within five feet of the foundation, with a $50 annual fee and a $300 refundable deposit, and the city inspects the line by video after you apply. Ask the city whether it handles the repair method, since the program describes the work as repair of defective lines and does not name lining.

For the part of the line the program does not cover, such as the section inside five feet of the foundation, lining may still be an option, and we plan around the boundary.
