About Florissant 24/7 Plumbing

A local plumbing line built around one promise: someone answers, any hour.

Call (314) 461-6239

Who we are

Florissant 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service line for Florissant, Missouri and the towns around it. We built the business around a phone line that is answered at all hours, because plumbing problems do not keep office hours.

We serve Florissant, Hazelwood, Ferguson, Black Jack, Bridgeton and Spanish Lake. Our work covers drain and sewer problems, water heaters, leaks, fixtures and sump systems for homes and small businesses.

How we work

We explain what we find in plain words and show you the problem when we can, for example with a drain camera recording. You decide what to fix.

  • Clear price before work starts.
  • Tell us when you need help and we explain timing.
  • A 24/7 emergency line for urgent problems.
  • Ask about permits and who pulls them before work starts.

Why local knowledge matters

Florissant's population rose from 1,369 in 1940 to 38,166 in 1960, so most of the housing here was built in a single wave. That means similar materials and similar failure points from street to street, and it lets us plan a visit around what is likely behind the wall. See our plumbing services or the towns we serve.

Missouri American Water reports total hardness up to 226 ppm at its Missouri River plants, so scale is a normal part of water heater life here. We build flushing and anode checks into our advice on water heaters, and we bring up a softener only when the problem calls for one.

What we do not do

Every trade has limits, and we state ours up front. We do not repair structures, pour concrete foundations or do electrical work beyond plumbing connections.

When a plumbing problem has a cause outside our trade, such as a bowed basement wall or a failed circuit, we say so and point you to the right professional. We also do not promise a fixed arrival time, because traffic, weather and call volume change by the hour. We give you an honest estimate of timing when you call.

How pricing works

You get a clear price before work starts, and any change in scope is agreed with you first. The price depends on the cause, the access, the parts and the time. We explain those factors in plain words, and our service pages list the main cost drivers for each job. If you want a number for your own house, call the line and describe the problem.

Following the guidelines of your own insurer matters, since insurance may cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but it often treats seepage and sewer backup differently. Read your policy and ask your agent, and keep our invoice and any photos for your claim.
