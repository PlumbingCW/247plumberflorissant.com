Tankless water heater service in Florissant, MO

Tankless water heater service in Florissant, MO covers three jobs: installing a new unit, repairing a unit that shows an error or turns off mid shower, and flushing scale out of the heat exchanger. A tankless heater only heats water as it flows, so scale and flow problems show up quickly.

People call us when a tankless unit cycles between hot and cold, throws a code, makes a clicking sound or has simply lost output. Many of those problems trace back to scale, a clogged inlet screen or a venting fault that can be fixed without replacing the unit.

Why descaling matters in this region

Missouri American Water's 2023 report lists total hardness up to 226 ppm at the Missouri River plants that supply much of St. Louis County. That is roughly 13 grains per gallon at the high end. Hard water leaves mineral scale in the heat exchanger of a tankless unit, which shortens flow and raises the temperature rise the unit must work for.

A yearly flush with a descaling solution is the standard guidance in hard water areas. We flush the unit, clean the inlet filter and check the burner or element while we are there.

Signs your tankless heater needs service

A service visit often costs far less than a replacement, which is why we start there.

  • Water runs hot, then cold, then hot again.
  • A fault code appears on the display.
  • The unit takes longer to deliver hot water than it used to.
  • You hear rumbling or clicking at startup.
  • It has not been flushed in more than a year.

Installing a tankless unit: what to check first

A tankless heater can use a larger gas line and a different venting material than the tank it replaces. We confirm those before you buy, so you do not find out on install day that the line is too small. We also check the water supply, the hot water demand in the house and the location for service access.

When a tankless unit is the wrong fit

A tankless heater does not suit every home. Long pipe runs, low flow and a very limited gas supply make a tank the better choice. We tell you that during the quote. If a tank makes more sense, our water heater installation team handles it. A unit that has failed completely is a replacement question.

Flushing and descaling

Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater, and Florissant's water is hard. A flush circulates a mild acid solution through the heat exchanger to dissolve scale. It takes a short visit when isolation valves are present, and it can restore flow and efficiency.

How often depends on use and water quality. A unit that shows error codes, takes longer to deliver hot water or makes noise may be scaled up. We check these signs on a service call and tell you what interval fits your house.

Sizing for your household

An undersized unit gives lukewarm water in a busy morning. If you are comparing it against a tank, see water heater installation and the cost factors in our tankless cost guide.

  • Count the fixtures that may run at the same time.
  • Match the unit's flow rate to the combined need.
  • Check the gas line capacity or electrical panel capacity.
  • Consider a recirculation line for long runs.
