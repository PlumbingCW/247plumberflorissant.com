Water line repair in Florissant, MO

Water line repair in Florissant, MO covers the service line that carries water from the utility's connection at the street to your house. A break there can show up as a wet or sunken spot in the yard, a drop in pressure, a spinning meter or a surprise water bill.

We repair a short break with a spot repair and replace long runs of failing pipe, using trenching or, where it suits, trenchless methods that disturb less of the yard. We also help you work out which part is yours and which belongs to the utility.

Service line break emergency

A broken service line can lose a lot of water quickly and flood a crawlspace or basement. If you can reach the shutoff valve at the meter, close it. If you cannot or are unsure, close the main inside the house and call us at any hour.

Watch for signs of a water main problem on the utility side as well, such as water bubbling up in the street or several neighbors with no water. In that case the utility, not your plumber, handles the main, and we help with anything on your side.

  • Close the main valve inside the house.
  • Note any wet or sunken ground and mark it.
  • Do not dig near the line until utilities are located.
  • Call us and tell us where the meter and the first interior shutoff are.

Low pressure: a service line symptom

A line that is partially blocked by scale or has a small leak can drop pressure without any visible wetness. We check pressure at an outside spigot, compare it to the pressure inside and test the pressure regulator, since a failing regulator causes the same symptoms. If the line is the culprit, we show you the numbers before you commit to a replacement.

Signs the service line is leaking

A line leak sends water into the ground where you cannot see it, so the bill is often the first clue. A leak detection visit locates the spot.

  • A meter that moves with every fixture off.
  • A wet or unusually green patch in the yard along the line.
  • Falling pressure through the whole house.
  • A sudden increase in the water bill.

Replacement methods

The line from the meter to the house is replaced by trench or by a trenchless pull, depending on the yard and the pipe. Trenchless methods pull a new line through the old path with small pits at each end, which keeps driveways and landscaping in place when conditions allow.

The meter belongs to the water utility, and work on the utility side of the meter is theirs. We handle the private side and coordinate where the two meet. Ask about permits before any digging begins, and call 811 so buried utilities are marked.
