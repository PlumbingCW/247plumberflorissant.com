Sump pump installation and repair in Florissant, MO

Sump pump service in Florissant, MO means restoring or replacing the pump that moves groundwater out of a basement pit before it can reach the floor. A sump system has a pit, a pump, a float switch, a discharge line and a check valve. Any one of those can fail.

We have not found a published groundwater figure for Florissant, so the right pump depends on what your own basement shows. If your basement takes on water, the first step is to find where it enters, then decide between a pump repair, a new pump and pit or exterior drainage work.

Sump pump emergency during heavy rain

If the pit is filling and the pump will not run, check that it is plugged in and that the breaker has not tripped. If it hums but does not pump, the impeller may be jammed. Do not stand in water near electrical outlets or equipment, and keep power off in a flooded basement.

Call us and tell us how high the water is in the pit. If it is close to overflowing, move valuables off the floor and use a wet vacuum or bucket to buy time, if it is safe to enter.

Backup power and protection

A pump on household power stops during a storm outage, which is when it is needed most. A battery backup pump or a water-powered backup keeps the pit under control until power returns. We discuss options and size the battery to the pit.

Discharge: where the water goes

The discharge line should carry water away from the house and not into the sanitary sewer. Pumping groundwater into the sewer is generally restricted. Ask the City of Florissant Public Works which rules apply to your lot before changing a discharge line. We route the line to a safe outlet and add a check valve to prevent backflow.

When a pump fails during a storm

Pumps fail at the worst moment because storms are when they work hardest. A stuck float, a clogged intake and a power outage are the usual causes. If your pump stops and water is rising, shut off power to anything in the water, move valuables up and call the line.

We can replace a failed pump on the visit in many cases, and we can add a battery backup and an alarm so the next storm does not catch you by surprise.

Questions to check about your pit

If any of these fails, the pump may be fine and the system is not. For related causes see basement waterproofing and leak detection.

  • Is the pit lined and covered?
  • Does the discharge pipe slope away from the foundation?
  • Is a check valve installed and working?
  • Does the pump run when you add a bucket of water?
